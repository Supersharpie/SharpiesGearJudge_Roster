# Sharpie's Gear Judge - Roster (Alt Upgrades) (Changelog)
---
## 🚀 v1.0.0
### 🎉 First Release
- **Alt upgrades on tooltips**: tradeable items list which of your other characters they upgrade, with the size of the upgrade (BIG / mid / small) and whether it's usable now or at a later level.
- **Roster grid**: a new tab in the Gear Judge window with every character's gear and slot scores side by side.
- **Check an item**: drag or shift-click an item onto the grid to see each character's upgrade and the slot it would replace.
- **Bags, bank and mail count**: items an alt is already holding for later count as owned. If they'll have something better by the time they can wear the new item, it isn't shown as an upgrade (the grid says "has better (bags)", "(bank)" or "(mail)"). Grid cells with a better item waiting are marked with a blue **+**. The bank and mailbox are saved each time that character opens them, and items you mail to one of your own characters count for them as soon as the mail is sent.
- **Short tooltips**: only the top 3 alts are listed by default (1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 on the Roster tab), and each alt has a checkbox to leave it off tooltips entirely.
- Bind-on-pickup and soulbound items show no Roster lines, since they can't be sent to an alt.
- Each character's spec, talents, level and tracked specs are saved when they log in, level, change talents or change gear.
- Options on the Roster tab: tooltip lines on/off, tradeable (BoE) items only, this realm and faction only, and whether to include items above an alt's level.
